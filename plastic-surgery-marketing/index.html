What's Costing You Consults

Marketing Mistakes Surgical Practices Make

Most plastic surgery practices waste budget on the same avoidable mistakes. Here's what we fix in the first 30 days of every engagement — and why it matters more when your average case is worth $10K+.

Slow Lead Follow-Up

A $12K case inquiry sits in an inbox for hours while the patient books with a faster competitor. Speed-to-lead is the single biggest leak in surgical practices.

Banned Ad Accounts

Running raw before/after photos and body-focused copy straight into Meta — and getting the ad account shut down. Compliance is non-negotiable for surgical advertising.

No Lead Qualification

Booking every click as a consult, then drowning the front desk in price-shoppers who never had the budget. Qualifying upfront protects your team's time.

Treating Surgery Like a Med Spa

Running flash-sale offers on a $15K procedure. Surgical patients buy on trust over weeks, not impulse. The wrong message repels your best cases.

One Generic "Procedures" Page

Listing every procedure on one page. Google and your funnel both need a dedicated, optimized page per procedure to rank and to convert high-intent traffic.

No Retargeting

Surgical patients rarely book on the first visit. Without retargeting, you pay to send researchers to a competitor who stays in front of them while you don't.

Vanity-Metric Reporting

Agencies reporting impressions and clicks instead of booked consults and surgical cases. If the report doesn't track revenue, it's hiding the truth.

Ignoring SEO Entirely

Relying 100% on paid ads. A plastic surgery SEO agency builds compounding consult flow that keeps coming long after ad spend stops.
