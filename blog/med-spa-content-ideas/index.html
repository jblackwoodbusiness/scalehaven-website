Med Spa Content Ideas: 30 Posts Patients Watch

By John Blackwood, Founder · August 22, 2026 · 9 min read

Here is how I think about organic content for a med spa: every post on Instagram or TikTok is a free lottery ticket. Most tickets do nothing, and that is fine. Then one video hits, a local woman watches it three times, decides your clinic is the one, and books. That patient cost you zero dollars. The clinics that win organic are simply the ones holding the most tickets.

The catch is that most clinics run out of ideas by week two and slide back to reposting product graphics, which nobody watches. So this is the idea bank: 30 specific posts, organized by category, almost all of them filmable on a phone in minutes. One rule sits above all of them: get a face on camera. People do not follow companies. They follow people. Every idea below works better with a human in the frame.

Provider-on-Camera Explainers (the trust builders)

These position you as the expert. Answer the questions you already hear in the treatment room every day.

  • 1. "What a first Botox appointment actually feels like," narrated start to finish
  • 2. "The question I get asked most about filler," answered straight to camera
  • 3. "Things I would never do to my own skin," from your injector or esthetician
  • 4. "How I decide how many units someone needs"
  • 5. "What nobody tells you about [treatment] recovery"
  • 6. "Myth or fact," rapid-fire on your most-booked treatment
  • 7. "How to spot a bad injector," an honest red-flags video
  • 8. Your provider reacting to a viral skincare trend with a professional take

Treatment-in-Action Videos (the scroll stoppers)

  • 9. A laser pass close up, with captions explaining what is happening
  • 10. Morpheus8 or microneedling in progress, narrated by the provider
  • 11. The body contouring machine visibly working
  • 12. A peel application from prep to finish in 20 seconds
  • 13. "Watch the whole appointment in 60 seconds" time-lapse
  • 14. Numbing to needle: the parts patients are nervous about, demystified

Always film with the patient’s written permission, keep other patients and charts out of frame, and save the dramatic transformation claims for the consult room. The treatment itself is interesting enough.

The Clinic and the Experience (the reassurance layer)

  • 15. A 30-second walkthrough of the space, front door to treatment room
  • 16. "What happens when you walk in," the full patient journey
  • 17. Room setup before an appointment, oddly satisfying and quietly professional
  • 18. The sterilization and safety routine nobody sees
  • 19. A day in the life at the front desk

Team and Personality (the follow reasons)

  • 20. Meet-the-team intros, one person per video, one fun fact each
  • 21. "What we would get done ourselves," each team member answers
  • 22. Behind the scenes of a team training day
  • 23. Your staff’s honest skincare routines
  • 24. Celebrating a milestone: an anniversary, a new device, a team member’s certification

Proof and Offers (the bookers)

  • 25. A patient testimonial in their own words, filmed with signed consent
  • 26. Before/after carousels with the treatment plan in the caption, consented and compliant
  • 27. "Why we built this offer," your provider explaining a named package and who it is for
  • 28. Event announcements as a personal invite from the owner, not a flyer
  • 29. "Spots this month" scarcity posts, used honestly and sparingly
  • 30. A monthly Q&A where you answer DM questions on camera
Real Result

This is not just brand-building. Your best organic videos are auditioning to become paid ads. In one live client account, provider-led creative behind a named Neurotoxin New Client Offer produced 128 leads at $3.83 per lead in 30 days. The content that wins the feed is the same content that wins the ad auction.

How to Actually Keep This Going

Batch it. One hour every two weeks, phone on a tripod, shoot five or six ideas from this list. Post three times a week minimum. Do not overthink polish: raw and real consistently outperforms produced and stiff in this niche, because authenticity is the product.

Then close the loop. Track which posts get watched and saved, and put ad budget behind the winners.
