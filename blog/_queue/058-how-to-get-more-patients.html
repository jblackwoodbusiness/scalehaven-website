How to Get More Patients: 12 Proven Ways

By John Blackwood, Founder · {{TODAY_HUMAN}} · 10 min read

Every private practice asks the same question sooner or later: how do we get more patients through the door? The encouraging answer is that patient acquisition follows the same physics whether you run a med spa, a dermatology clinic, a plastic surgery practice, or a dental office. Patients discover you, validate you, and book you, and every reliable growth lever strengthens one of those three steps.

Here are the 12 that actually move the number, ordered roughly from free-and-fast to paid-and-scalable.

Fix What You Already Have (Free, Do These First)

1. Complete your Google Business Profile. For "near me" searches, the map pack is the whole first page. Fill every field, pick the right categories, add real photos monthly, and list services individually. This single free asset outperforms most paid channels for local practices.

2. Build review velocity. A steady stream of new reviews beats a large stale total. Ask at checkout when satisfaction peaks, follow with a one-tap link by text, and never confirm someone is a patient when you reply. Two to three new reviews a week changes your local rankings within months.

3. Answer faster than everyone. Around 78% of patients book with whoever responds first, and a five-minute response converts at multiples of a one-hour response. Route every inquiry to a human immediately and give your front desk a response-time standard. This is the cheapest growth lever in this entire list.

4. Make booking effortless. Online scheduling linked from your website, your Google profile, your social bios, and every follow-up message. Each extra step between "I want this" and a confirmed slot loses patients you already earned.

Turn Existing Patients Into More Patients

5. Run a double-sided referral program. Reward the referrer and the friend. One-sided offers ask patients to profit from a friendship; double-sided offers let them give a gift. Make it grabbable: cards at checkout, a line in your post-visit text.

6. Reactivate your lapsed list. The cheapest new appointment is a patient who already knows you. A simple email or text campaign to everyone who has not visited in 6-12 months, with a clear reason to return, reliably fills slow weeks.

7. Rebook before they leave. Whatever your specialty, prescribe the next visit in the chair with a clinical reason attached. Practices that systematize rebooking need dramatically less new-patient marketing.

Get Found by New Patients

8. Build a page for every service. One generic services page competes for nothing. A dedicated, genuinely useful page per treatment or procedure wins the specific searches patients actually type, and it gives your ads better landing pages too.

9. Put real faces on camera. People book people, not logos. Providers on camera explaining procedures, showing the space, and answering common questions build trust before the first visit. It powers organic social and it is the highest-converting ad creative in every vertical we run.

10. Run search ads on high-intent terms. When someone searches a procedure plus your city, they are choosing a provider now. Being at the top of that page is purchasable, and for high-value services the click cost is trivial next to patient value.

11. Create demand on social with a named offer. Meta and Instagram ads reach the much larger group who are not searching yet. The formula that works across specialties: a specific, named entry-point offer plus authentic on-camera creative, qualified with form questions so your team only talks to real prospects.

Real Result

From a live client account, one 30-day window: a Neurotoxin New Client Offer brought in 128 leads at $3.83 per lead from under $500 in spend, and a Glo2Facial campaign added 69 leads at $4.66. About 200 qualified leads for roughly $810, every one routed straight to the clinic’s team the moment it landed.

12. Partner where your patients already are. Gyms and trainers for body treatments, salons for aesthetics, physicians for referral-driven specialties. One well-chosen local partnership can feed a practice for years at zero media cost.

The Order That Works

Weeks one and two: profile, reviews, response speed, booking friction (items 1-4). They are free and they multiply everything else. Weeks three and four: referral and reactivation (5-7), because warm revenue funds cold acquisition. Then build the visibility layer (8-12) one channel at a time until you are present everywhere a patient could look for you.
