Why Clinics Fire Their Marketing Agency

When I ask owners what went wrong with their last agency, the answers come down to two things.

  • No results. Not "results were slow". Months of retainer and ad spend with no measurable lift in booked consultations. The reports looked busy, the calendar did not change.
  • The agency did not understand aesthetics. Most of these were general Google Ads or Meta Ads shops that also work with roofers, dentists and restaurants. They ran the playbook that works in those industries and learned the hard way that a med spa is different: different ad policies, different buyers, different economics per patient.

What owners say they want next time is just as consistent. Someone who knows the industry. Results they can measure in booked patients. Reporting they can actually read, with real access to their own numbers. Transparency about what is being spent and why. And the ability to talk to the person running their account, not a coordinator who passes messages along. That list is the backbone of the criteria below.

How to Judge Any Med Spa Marketing Company

  • Niche depth. Aesthetics has its own physics: ad platform restrictions on before/afters, HIPAA exposure, treatment seasonality, price-shopper traps. A generalist learns on your budget.
  • Proof with numbers. Ask for real campaign results with costs per lead and per booked consultation. "We increased engagement" is not an answer.
  • Skin in the game. Who carries the risk of a slow first month: you or them? Guarantees are rare in this industry, which tells you something.
  • What they make you install. Some companies are software platforms first and agencies second. If the pitch requires migrating your booking system or adopting a new CRM, understand you are buying lock-in with your marketing.
  • Contract terms. 90-day initial commitments are reasonable because campaigns need a ramp. Twelve-month locks with auto-renewal before any results are not.
  • Transparency and access. You should own your ad accounts, see the spend and results whenever you want, and be able to reach the person managing your campaigns directly.
  • Reporting you can read. A live dashboard you can open any time, showing leads, booked consultations and cost per booking for every channel. A PDF once a month is not enough, and if the report leads with impressions and reach, it is hiding something.

What a Fair Med Spa Marketing Agency Contract Looks Like

Expect a minimum term of three months from almost every serious agency, and some ask for longer. We do the same: a three-month initial commitment. That is not a lock-in tactic. It is how long the work genuinely takes.

  • Month one: campaigns launch, Google and Meta gather data, and the first offers and creative get tested.
  • Month two: budget moves to what is working. Weak ads, keywords and audiences get cut.
  • Month three: the optimized account runs at full speed, and SEO starts to show traction in the rankings.

A month-to-month deal sounds safer, but no agency can optimize Google Ads, Meta Ads and SEO properly in 30 days, so you are paying for a launch and walking away before the payoff. What you should push back on is a long term with no accountability in it. Before you sign anything, check three things:

  • Who owns the ad accounts, the website and the data if you part ways. The answer should be you.
  • What happens if the first month underdelivers. Most agencies say "give it time". We put it in writing: 15+ booked consultations in month one, or we keep working free until you hit it.
  • What happens after the initial term. Avoid twelve-month auto-renewals that kick in before you have seen results.

The Five Questions That Expose Any Agency

  • What software will you require me to adopt, and what happens to my data if we part ways?
  • Can I speak to a current client with a practice like mine?

Med Spa Marketing Agency FAQ

What does a med spa marketing agency do?

It brings new patients into your clinic, usually through Meta Ads, Google Ads and SEO. A good one also builds the offers, the ad creative and the lead forms, routes leads to your team quickly, and reports on booked consultations rather than clicks.

How much does a med spa marketing agency cost?

Most specialist agencies charge $2,500 to $5,000 USD a month for management, with ad spend paid separately to Meta and Google. Plan on at least $1,500 a month in Meta spend for campaigns to learn properly.

How long are med spa marketing agency contracts?

Usually a three-month minimum, sometimes longer. Three months gives an agency time to launch, optimize Google and Meta, and start building SEO traction. Be wary of twelve-month terms that auto-renew before you have seen results.

Should I hire a general marketing agency or an aesthetics specialist?

A specialist. The most common reason owners give for firing an agency is that it did not understand the industry. Aesthetics has its own ad policies, its own buyers and its own economics, and a generalist learns all of that on your budget.

How fast should a med spa marketing agency get results?

Leads should start within days of launch, and booked consultations within the first month if your team calls leads back quickly. Ranking from SEO takes longer, usually several months. If an agency cannot tell you what month one should look like, keep looking.

What should I ask before hiring a med spa marketing agency?

Ask for cost per booked consultation on recent campaigns, who makes the creative, who owns your ad accounts, whether you get a live dashboard, what happens if month one underdelivers, and whether you can talk to the person running your account. The five questions above cover it.
