About ScaleHaven

Built by an Operator,
Not a Career Marketer

Most agencies learned aesthetics from the outside. ScaleHaven was built from inside a clinic — one that grew into one of the largest in its region and sold to private equity.

The Story Behind ScaleHaven

ScaleHaven's founder, John Blackwood, didn't start in marketing. He started in a cosmetic clinic — a family business that had to figure out, treatment by treatment and patient by patient, what actually fills an aesthetic practice's calendar.

That clinic grew into one of the largest in its region. The playbook that grew it wasn't complicated, but it was disciplined: named treatment offers instead of discounts, relentless speed on every lead, retention systems that turned one visit into years of visits, and marketing measured in booked consultations — never impressions. Eventually the clinic did what well-built businesses do: it attracted a buyer, and sold to a private equity firm.

ScaleHaven exists to run that same playbook for med spas, aesthetic clinics, and plastic surgery practices across the US and Canada. Not theory from a marketing textbook — the operating system of a clinic that actually won.

What We Believe (and Build Into Every Engagement)

  • Offers beat discounts. A named, specific offer attracts patients who stay. "$8 Botox" attracts price shoppers who churn. We saw this from inside the clinic; now it's the first thing we fix for clients.
  • Speed decides revenue. Roughly 78% of patients book with whoever responds first. Every ScaleHaven system starts with speed: leads routed to your team the second they arrive, a 5-minute-response playbook, and self-booking funnels that put patients straight on your calendar — with full follow-up automation available as an add-on.
  • Real faces convert. Patients choose people, not logos. We put providers on camera because authenticity breaks the cold-ad barrier better than any production budget.
  • One metric matters. Booked consultations. Not reach, not clicks, not likes. If it doesn't put a patient in a chair, we don't report it as a win.
Proof, Not Promises

For a Greater Toronto Area med spa, we built a curated PRP hair-restoration offer and ran it on Meta. A $1,000 ad budget produced 100+ leads at roughly $10 each — 10 booked consultations, 5 closed packages, about $12,500 in month-one revenue. No procedure video existed; image ads and a strong offer carried it. That campaign is the ScaleHaven method in miniature.

For a Texas med spa, we ran Google and Meta together with named offers and zero-integration lead routing. $2,500 in ad spend became $28,000 in first-visit revenue in the first 30 days, at about $50 per new patient.

The Guarantee That Makes It Real

Because the system is proven, we put our fee where our confidence is: 15+ booked consultations in your first month, or we work free until you hit it. No agency that merely "manages ads" can offer that. An operator who has filled a clinic's calendar before can.
