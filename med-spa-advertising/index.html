The Channels We Run

Two Channels. One Booked Calendar.

There are two paid channels that consistently book patients for med spas. Meta creates demand. Google captures it. The clinics that win run both — and we manage every dollar across both, end to end.

01

Meta Ads — Facebook & Instagram

Meta is the volume engine for med spa advertising. Before-and-after transformation videos, treatment offers, and qualifying lead forms put your clinic in front of a hyper-local audience scrolling Instagram and Facebook. It's the best channel for visual treatments — Botox, fillers, laser, body contouring, and skin — because the result sells itself. This is where most clinics get their lowest cost per lead.

  • Retargeting that re-engages anyone who didn't book first time

Our Advertising System

From Ad Spend to Booked Patients

Anyone can run ads. Booking patients takes a system. Every med spa advertising campaign we run is built on four pillars that turn a cold scroll or a search into a confirmed consultation.

01

Creative That Stops the Scroll

The ad is everything. You film raw content at your clinic — we script, edit, and produce scroll-stopping before-and-after videos, offer-driven ads, and copy that speaks to the patient's result, not your process. Weak creative is the number-one reason med spa ads fail. We make creative the strength.

  • Scripting and editing from your raw clinic footage
  • Before-and-after transformation ads that convert
  • Offer-led copy written to book, not just to like
02

Targeting That Reaches Buyers

The best ad shown to the wrong person is wasted spend. We target by location radius, treatment intent, demographics, and search behavior — then layer in retargeting and lookalike audiences. On Google, we bid on the exact buyer-intent keywords patients use the moment they're ready to book.

  • Hyper-local radius targeting around your clinic
  • Buyer-intent keyword targeting on Google Search
  • Lookalike + retargeting audiences to lower cost per booking
03

Lead Capture That Qualifies

A click means nothing until it becomes a qualified lead. We use higher-intent Instant Forms and conversion-optimized landing pages with qualifying questions — so the leads that come through are people who can actually afford the treatment and are ready to book. Fewer junk leads, more real consultations.

  • Qualifying questions filter out tire-kickers
  • Landing pages built to convert search and social traffic
  • Leads flow straight into your CRM in real time
04

Automated Follow-Up

Most leads never book because nobody follows up fast enough. Our system texts and emails every new lead within 60 seconds, then keeps nurturing until they book. This is the difference between a lead and a patient — and the reason our cost per booked consultation stays low. Read more on our lead generation system.
